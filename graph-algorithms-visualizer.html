Which one should I use?

If the question asks…UseReason
“Which place has the fewest stops?”BFSIt explores nearest vertices first.
“Can I reach this place?” or “Explore deeply”DFSIt follows one path fully before backtracking.
“Which route has the lowest distance or cost?”DijkstraIt uses edge weights, unlike BFS and DFS.
“How can I connect every location most cheaply?”Prim’s MSTIt creates a minimum-cost network, not a route.
“Does this network contain a loop?”Cycle checkIt identifies circular paths.
“How many separate networks are there?”ComponentsIt groups all reachable vertices together.

Easy conclusion: BFS, DFS, and Dijkstra help us travel through a graph. Prim’s MST helps us build a cheap network. Cycle detection and components help us understand the graph’s structure.
